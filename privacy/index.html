Privacy Policy for Podly

Effective date: 2026-11-01

Contact: nikhilkumarreddypyreddy@gmail.com

Podly is an Android app that connects over Bluetooth to your paired AirPods so you can see their battery and ear status and change their settings. This policy explains what information Podly handles and what it does not. The short version: Podly does not collect, send or share any personal information.

Summary

  • Podly has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network access.
  • Everything Podly knows stays on your phone.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • You can delete everything Podly stores at any time by clearing its data or uninstalling it.

Information Podly handles

Podly does not collect personal information. To work, it handles the following on your device only:

  • The name and Bluetooth address of the AirPods you choose, so it can reconnect to them.
  • Your choices in the app, such as automatic connection, button actions and display options.
  • A short local log of connection events (for example, "connected" or "AirPods disconnected"), shown in the app so you can see why a connection did or did not happen.
  • Battery levels, in-ear status and settings reported by your AirPods. These are shown on screen while you use the app and are not stored or sent anywhere.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. App backups are turned off, so it is not copied to cloud backups by Podly.

How Podly uses information

Only to run the features you use: connecting to your AirPods, showing their status, applying the settings you choose, and pausing or resuming media when you take your AirPods out or put them back in. Podly does not use information for advertising, profiling, analytics or any purpose other than operating the app on your device.

Sharing and third parties

Podly does not share information with anyone. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it has no internet permission, so it cannot send information off your phone. Podly does not sell or share personal information.

Where information is stored and how long

Information stays on your phone until you clear the app's data, uninstall Podly, or remove the AirPods from the app. Podly keeps nothing on any server, so there is no data held elsewhere to delete or transfer, and no international transfer of data.

Permissions and why Podly asks for them

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect): to connect to your already paired AirPods.
  • Notifications: to show the ongoing "connected" notification and connection hints.
  • Foreground service (connected device): to keep the Bluetooth connection running while you use your AirPods.
  • Companion device and background start: so Android can start Podly when your AirPods connect, after you set up automatic connection once.
  • Receive boot completed: to check after your phone restarts whether your AirPods are already connected.

Podly does not request internet, location, contacts, microphone, camera, storage, or phone permissions. You can revoke any permission in Android settings. Some features will stop working without the permission they need.

Google Play

You install Podly through Google Play. Google may collect and process information about your use of Google Play and your device under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). That is separate from Podly, and the developer of Podly does not receive that information from Podly.

Your rights and choices

Because Podly does not collect or hold your personal information, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You control all of it directly on your phone: change settings in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall Podly. If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or another place with privacy rights, those rights apply, and you can contact the address below with any request or question. Podly does not make automated decisions about you, does not sell or share personal information, and does not use sensitive personal information.

If you contact the developer

If you email the address below, the developer will use your email address and your message only to reply and will keep them only as long as needed to handle your request.

Children

Podly is a general utility and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Security

Information stays in the app's private storage protected by Android's app sandbox. Podly talks only to your paired AirPods over Bluetooth and sends nothing over the internet. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone updated and protected with a screen lock.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version and its effective date will be published at this address. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's Google Play listing.
